# Outlier Swarm — Guide

## Why This Matters

Most research tells you what everyone already knows. Outlier Swarm finds what everyone is missing.

Inspired by Tim Ferriss's approach — instead of asking "who is the best?", ask "who ignores the standard doctrine and still wins?" — this skill deploys five researchers with five different mandates, all running in parallel. Then it looks for the signal: where do two or three of them independently point to the same surprising conclusion?

That convergence — "overlaps of surprise" — is where the real insight hides.

## When to Use It

Outlier Swarm is your go-to when you're questioning conventional wisdom:

- **"What's everyone missing about X?"** — Challenge assumptions in your niche
- **"Is there a better way?"** — Question established practices
- **"Who does it differently and still wins?"** — Find contrarian approaches worth stealing
- **"Why does everyone say X?"** — Map the sacred cows before deciding whether to follow them

**Real examples:**
- "Outlier swarm on group coaching pricing — is the $5K minimum really necessary?"
- "What's everyone missing about client onboarding for coaching businesses?"
- "Find contrarian approaches to building an email list"
- "Question the conventional wisdom on webinars for high-ticket coaching"

## The Five Lenses

Every Outlier Swarm deploys the same five researchers:

1. **Mainstream Mapper** — Maps what everyone believes (you need to know the rules before you break them)
2. **Contrarian Hunter** — Finds people who do the opposite and still get results
3. **Cross-Domain Thief** — Steals solutions from completely different fields
4. **First Principles Assassin** — Strips away tradition to find what actually causes results
5. **Failure Archaeologist** — Digs up ideas that were abandoned too early

## What to Expect

**Time:** 2-5 minutes (all 5 agents run in parallel)

**Output:** A structured report with:
- Sacred cows identified (what the mainstream believes without questioning)
- Contrarian signals (who breaks the rules and wins)
- Cross-domain connections (solutions borrowed from other fields)
- First principles analysis (what's tradition vs. what's necessary)
- Ideas worth resurrecting (abandoned approaches that deserve a second look)
- **Overlaps of Surprise** — the convergence matrix showing where 2+ angles agree
- Mission relevance section tailored to YOUR coaching business

**Cost:** Minimal — all 5 agents use haiku. The insight comes from the framework, not compute power.

## How It Gets Smarter With Your Context

If you've built your mission context (`/mission-context-builder`), every agent gets a line about who you are and who you serve. This means:

- The Contrarian Hunter looks for rule-breakers in YOUR specific niche
- The Cross-Domain Thief finds analogies relevant to YOUR type of coaching
- The Mission Relevance section maps findings to YOUR practice, YOUR clients, YOUR frameworks

No mission context? It still works — you just get generic contrarian research instead of niche-specific insights.

## Research Swarm vs Outlier Swarm

Use **Research Swarm** when you need to understand something thoroughly — "What are my options?"

Use **Outlier Swarm** when you need to challenge something — "What is everyone getting wrong?"

They complement each other. Research Swarm maps the territory. Outlier Swarm questions the map.

## Tips

- **Name the assumption:** "Everyone says coaches need webinars — outlier swarm that" gets sharper results than "research webinars"
- **Follow the convergence:** If 3 of the 5 agents independently point to the same thing, pay attention
- **Use the Failure Archaeologist findings:** Abandoned ideas in a new context are often the highest-leverage insights
- **Don't automatically adopt contrarian positions:** The skill surfaces contrarian evidence. You decide whether it applies to your situation.
