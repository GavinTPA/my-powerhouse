# setup-vault — What It Does and Why It Matters

## The One-Liner

Type "set up my vault" into your Powerhouse and Claude configures your Obsidian Knowledge Vault — daily note template, folder structure, and semantic search — in about two minutes.

## Why This Exists

Your Knowledge Vault is where everything your Powerhouse produces ends up. Research swarms, newsletter drafts, coaching session summaries, YouTube notes — they all save to your vault automatically, with labels, with links.

Without a configured vault, that output has nowhere to land. And without the daily note, you don't have a command center to see what's happening each day.

This skill handles the setup so you don't have to click through 7 different Obsidian settings screens.

## What It Sets Up

**Your daily note template** — Every day, Obsidian creates a fresh note with:
- Ship This = Win Day (your ONE focus)
- Today's 3 (three tasks, no more)
- Captures (everything that comes in)
- Done Today (your victory lap)
- Scratch (thinking space)

**Your folder structure** — Templates/ for your templates, Zettelkasten/ for all your notes.

**Semantic search for Claude Code** — So when you ask Claude a question, it checks YOUR vault first — your knowledge, your decisions, your frameworks — before searching the web.

## How to Use It

Open your Powerhouse terminal and say:

```
set up my vault
```

Claude checks what's already in place, sets up what's missing, and tells you exactly what to do next (two restarts: Obsidian and Claude Code).

## After Setup

1. Close and reopen Obsidian
2. Click the calendar icon in the sidebar — your first daily note appears
3. Type `/exit` then `claude` to reconnect Claude Code
4. Try: "search my vault for daily notes"

That's it. Your Knowledge Vault is live.
