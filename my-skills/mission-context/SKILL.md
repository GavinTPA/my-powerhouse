## WHO I AM

**Gavin Smith** - Profit and cash flow educator who gives business owners the tools and confidence to run their own numbers — without relying on an accountant.

**Niche:** $1M–$5M businesses across any industry. Niched by problem, not sector: profit, cash flow, and understanding the numbers that drive both. The client trigger is the "readiness moment" — when an owner finally decides to stop outsourcing their financial understanding and take ownership themselves.

**What clients get:** The Profit and Cash Flow Accelerator — a 90-day program (because "90 days is a lot faster than three months"). Clients access 9 core modules on a School platform, 1:1 and weekly/monthly group coaching calls, and done-for-you delivery of four tools: a Weekly Cash Flow Forecaster, a Monthly Clarity Report, a Profit Calculator, and a Five Year Forecast. After the program, clients continue on a monthly retainer — tools maintained, coaching calls retained.

**Superpower:** In under 30 minutes, Gavin can look at any business's numbers and identify exactly what their next right move is, what to focus on to improve profit, and precisely what's driving cash flow. His methodology — structured around decision-making for business owners, not accounting or reporting — teaches clients to do the same for themselves. No accounting jargon. No dependence on external financial experts. Just clear, actionable insight from the numbers that actually matter.

**Background:** 25+ year career in finance. Chartered Accountant (KPMG), then 10 years rising to International CFO of a global marketing agency. Last 8 years as a business owner: bought a bar doing $1.1M/year and losing $150K, grew it into The Beach Bars group (3 bars, thebeachbars.com.au) doing $11.5M with $1.5M profit. Built and sold a chain of Pilates studios. This coaching business is the third — built from startup to $1M+.

**The relatability edge:** Not an accountant who coaches. A business owner who happens to have a CFO brain. Has lived the highs and lows and is still on that journey. That's what makes this land differently with clients.

**Revenue:** ~$1M AUD annually ($85–100K AUD/month). Based in Australia.

## MY CLIENTS

**Ideal Client:** Larissa. She owns a $1.2M revenue business with very low margins. She inherited full responsibility for the business after a relationship separation — her husband had handled the numbers, and suddenly she was accountable for finances she didn't understand. She represents a pattern: business owners who've been running blind and have hit their readiness moment.

**Surface problem:** The cash flow rollercoaster — high balances one week, near-zero the next, a tax bill that wipes everything out. They run the business all year and then wait for the accountant to tell them whether they made a profit. They don't know what to focus on. They feel like they're not making enough money despite the work they're putting in.

**Deeper reality:** There are usually 1-2 specific things they should be focusing on to improve profit and scale. They don't need more information — they need a simple framework that shows them what matters and what to do next. Once they have it, the cash flow stress disappears because they can see what's coming long in advance.

**Success looks like:** It's a Tuesday and they're deciding whether to hire a new employee, increase marketing spend, sign a lease on bigger premises, or commit to a new piece of equipment. Six months ago they would have second-guessed it, stressed about it, maybe said no out of fear. Today they pull the trigger — not recklessly, but with total confidence. They've looked at the numbers. They know they can afford it. They understand the risk and the reward. They decide faster and with more clarity.

**Their words:**
- "I had cash, but then the tax bill came and wiped it out"
- "I know we should invest in marketing, but how much can I actually afford?"
- "The business has grown but profit hasn't, and I'm not sure why"
- "My accountant says I made a profit but it's not in my bank account"
- "We never seem to have the money we should have"

**The hidden emotion:** They're performing success on the outside — they've built a business, they have a team, they look the part. Internally they feel shame and embarrassment that they don't understand their own numbers. They carry the weight of their team and clients depending on them, while quietly not knowing if the business is actually okay.

**Where they hang out:** Typically inside other business coaching programs (growth-minded but those programs don't cover finance). Consuming business growth content, not finance content — because finance feels boring and irrelevant until it suddenly isn't. *(Specific communities TBC)*

## MY VOICE

**Style:** Clear, direct, grounded

**How I communicate:**
- Plain English always — no accounting jargon
- Decision-making framing, not reporting framing
- Direct and momentum-forward — make shit happen, not stop it
- Empowerment-focused: the goal is always owner independence, not expert dependency
- 80% is good enough — don't over-analyse, just act

**Signature phrases:**
- "80% is good enough"
- "Insights and actions, not reporting and over-analysis"
- "Focus on what matters"
- "Financial clarity first"
- "Clarity, confidence and growth" — the three phases of the client journey

**Words I avoid:**
- Sentences starting with "Most businesses..." — dead giveaway it's AI-written
- Em-dashes in writing — another AI tell
- "And here's the kicker..." — ChatGPT fingerprint, never use this
- "Profit First" — never reference or recommend; it's disempowering and a handbrake for business owners
- Fractional CFO / Virtual CFO / outsourcing financial leadership — goes against the core belief that business owners should own their numbers themselves

**Influences:**
- **Lord Chadlington** (early mentor, UK): "The point of life is to make shit happen, not fucking stop it." — the source of the decisive, momentum-forward energy in how Gavin coaches and communicates
- **Taki Moore** (current coach): approach to life and business — how Gavin thinks about coaching and building a business

## MY TERMINOLOGY

| Term | What It Means |
|------|---------------|
| **Clarity, Confidence and Growth** | The three phases of the client journey and the outcomes of the methodology. Simplify numbers → Clarity. Know your numbers → Confidence. Use your numbers → Growth. |
| **The Profit Analyst Method** | The overarching name for the full methodology. Structured around decision-making for business owners — not accounting, compliance, or reporting. Named for the business: The Profit Analyst. |
| **The Profit Formula** | The signature move. Analyses exactly what drives a business's profit, identifies the hidden opportunities, and shows owners precisely what to focus on to improve profitability. Comparing current vs. target profit formula reveals the next right moves. |
| **Weekly Cash Flow Forecaster** | Tool 1. A weekly forward-looking view of cash so owners can always see their next low point in advance and act before it becomes a crisis. |
| **Monthly Clarity Report** | Tool 2. Monthly view of P&L and balance sheet in plain English — the numbers that matter, not an accounting dump. |
| **Profit Calculator** | Tool 3. Analyses the current profit formula and identifies exactly what the next right move is to improve profitability. |
| **Five Year Forecast** | Tool 4. Shows the long-term direction and impact on P&L and balance sheet of decisions being made today. |
| **The Six Strategic Questions** | The questions every business owner should run on repeat — two each from cash flow, profitability, and balance sheet. The rhythm that keeps owners connected to their numbers without becoming accountants. |
| **The Cash Flow Questions** | Q1: "When is our next low point and how low is it?" Q2: "How much cash is available for investment decisions?" |
| **The Profit Questions** | Q1: "What is my current profit formula?" Q2: "What is my target profit formula?" — comparing the two reveals exactly what to focus on. |
| **The Balance Sheet Questions** | Q1: "Where is my profit going?" Q2: "What is my current financial position and resources?" |
| **Owe and Own** | Plain-English replacement for liabilities and assets. Used to make balance sheet concepts accessible to owners who've never engaged with accounting language. |
| **The Readiness Moment** | The trigger point when a business owner finally decides to stop outsourcing their financial understanding and take ownership themselves. This is the ideal client entry point. |
