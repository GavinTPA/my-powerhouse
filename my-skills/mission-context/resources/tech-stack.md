## How They Connect

```
You <-> Claude Code <-> Your Powerhouse (GitHub)
                   <-> Your Notes (Obsidian)
                   <-> Your Skills (my-skills/)
```

Claude Code sits at the center. It reads your CLAUDE.md to understand you, uses your skills to do work, and saves results to your files.
