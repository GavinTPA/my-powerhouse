# Nano Banana Pro - How It Works

## The One-Sentence Version

Need an image? Just describe it and this skill generates it using Google's best image AI.

## Why This Exists

Think of all the times you're working on something and suddenly need a visual:
- Writing an article? Need a hero image.
- Creating a presentation? Need a slide graphic.
- Posting on social? Need an eye-catching image.

Before this skill, you'd have to stop, open AI Studio, craft a prompt, generate, download, find the file... the whole flow breaks.

Now? Just say "generate an image of [whatever]" and it appears in your Downloads folder. Done.

## How You Use It

**The simple version:**
> "Generate an image of a lighthouse cutting through fog"

That's it. You get a 16:9 hero image at 1K resolution.

**With more control:**
> "Create a 4K image of me presenting to a small audience, professional photography style"

This uses your reference photo, outputs at 4K, and applies a photography style.

**Different formats:**
> "Make a 1:1 thumbnail of a lightbulb moment"

Square format for profiles or thumbnails.

## The "Me" Shortcut

Say "me", "image of me", or "myself" and the skill automatically uses your reference photo. No need to specify paths.

## Style Lives in the Prompt

Unlike other tools with preset "styles", here you just describe the style you want:
- "professional photography style"
- "manga illustration"
- "minimalist graphic design"
- "watercolor painting"

This gives you unlimited flexibility. And when you find a style that works great, we add it to the style library so you can reference it again.

## The Analogy

Think of this skill like having a professional photographer/illustrator on speed dial. You don't hand them a spec sheet with 47 parameters. You say "I need a hero shot that captures the feeling of clarity breaking through confusion" and they figure out the lighting, composition, and style.

That's what the prompting best practices baked into this skill do - they translate your intent into what the AI needs to hear.

## What It Costs

About 13 cents per image at standard resolution. A dollar or so for a set of 8 carousel images. Cheaper than any stock photo subscription and every image is custom-made for your business, your brand, your message.

You need a Google billing account to use image generation through the API (one-time setup). There's no subscription — you pay per image, and at 13 cents each, you'd have to generate 150 images to spend $20. Type `nano banana costs` to see your running total anytime.

**First-time setup:** Type `set up nano banana pro` and Claude walks you through getting your API key and connecting everything. Takes about 10 minutes.
