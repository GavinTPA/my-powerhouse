# Powerhouse QA — Guide

## What This Is

Your quality checker. Before anything goes live — content, tools, documents — run it through Powerhouse QA. It reads your mission-context to understand your business, then reviews through that lens.

Think of it as a trusted colleague who reads everything before it ships and says "here's what I'd fix."

## Why It Matters

AI tools produce output fast. Fast output that hasn't been checked is a liability. Powerhouse QA catches:

- **Content that doesn't sound like you** — AI slop, generic language, wrong tone
- **Documents that would embarrass you** — people described in ways they wouldn't choose, private observations made public
- **Tools that aren't ready** — missing pieces, edge cases, things that break with real data
- **Risks you haven't thought of** — security gaps, cost exposure, reputation risks

## How to Use It

Just tell it what to review and how:

- **"Review this content"** — checks voice, audience fit, AI slop
- **"Team safety audit on this document"** — checks if people described would be comfortable reading it
- **"Is this ready to ship?"** — checks for edge cases, failures, missing pieces
- **"Red team this"** — looks for security issues, abuse potential, cost risks
- **"How would a user experience this?"** — walks through as a first-time user

## The Team Safety Audit

This is the one most coaches haven't thought about. When you build tools that describe your team, your clients, or your partners — would those people be comfortable seeing what you've written?

Common issues it catches:
- Rating or grading people by name
- Quoting private conversations in permanent documents
- Describing someone's work as a problem to fix rather than work being done
- Attaching performance data to named individuals

Every issue comes with a neutral rewrite that keeps the operational information without the uncomfortable framing.

## Tips

1. **Build your mission-context first.** The reviews are dramatically better when the skill knows your voice, your clients, and your terminology.
2. **Run Team Safety Audit on anything your team will see.** Once. Before they see it.
3. **Don't fix everything at once.** BLOCKERs first. Advisory items second. Watch items when you have time.
4. **The skill is constructive, not critical.** Every issue comes with a suggested fix. It's not about what's wrong — it's about making it better.
