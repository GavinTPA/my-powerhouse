# Powerhouse QA — Roadmap

## What's Shipped

| Version | Date | What Changed |
|---------|------|--------------|
| v1.0 | 2026-03-24 | Initial release. Five review modes (Content QA, Team Safety Audit, Pre-Ship Check, Red Team, User Perspective). Mission-context integration. Structured output format with severity levels. |

## Planned Improvements

- [ ] **Review templates per content type** — Specific checklists for offer docs, email campaigns, workshop materials, social posts
- [ ] **Batch review mode** — Review multiple files in one pass with a summary report
- [ ] **Pre-publish checklist** — Standard checks before posting to external platforms (Instagram, Substack, LinkedIn)

## Ideas (Not Committed)

- Integration with ai-slop-detector for automated slop scoring
- History tracking — what issues keep recurring across reviews
- Client-specific safety profiles (which team members see which tools)

## What We've Learned

The CC Mission Context Casper Audit (2026-03-13) proved that Team Safety Audit is high-value. 13 issues found across 4 files — 4 were blockers that would have caused real problems if team members had seen them. The neutral rewrites preserved all operational information while removing uncomfortable framing.
