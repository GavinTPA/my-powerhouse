# From Article to Instagram Carousel

**macOS only.** Not supported on Windows.

## Getting Started — Setup Sequence

1. **"set up instagram carousel"** — Add your reference photo + pick your visual style (5 min)
2. **"set up instagram poster"** — Connect your Instagram account for direct posting (10 min)
3. **"set up cutout"** — Install background removal for transparent logos (5 min, optional)

You can create carousels (text + prompts) without any setup.
Image generation requires step 1.
Posting requires steps 1 + 2.

## How It Works

Say "create a carousel from [article/topic]" and the agent handles everything:
- Extracts the core message from your article
- Writes 6-8 slides with real teaching
- Picks a visual metaphor that fits your content
- Generates image prompts optimized for AI generation
- Evaluates and sharpens your hook automatically
- Writes the Instagram caption
- Saves everything to your vault (or Downloads)

## Visual Styles

Your carousel images use a visual style — the look and feel of every slide. You pick a default style during setup, and the agent uses it automatically.

**Built-in styles:** Manga, Cinematic Photo, Watercolor Editorial, Minimalist Concept, Bold Graphic.

Want something custom? Just describe it and it gets added to your style library. Say "I want my carousels to look like vintage travel posters" and you'll get a new style.

## Image Generation

After the carousel text is ready, say "generate images" to create the actual slide images. The AI draws each scene, maintaining character consistency across all 8 slides using chat mode.

Cost: about $1.07 per carousel (8 images).

## Reference Photos

If you want to appear as a character in your carousels, add a headshot during setup. The AI draws you into the scenes — giving a personal feel to your brand content.

## Posting

Once images are generated, say "post this carousel to Instagram" and it goes live. The posting skill handles uploading, captioning, and publishing.
