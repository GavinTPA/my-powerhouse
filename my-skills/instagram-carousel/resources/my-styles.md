# My Visual Styles

## How to Add a Style
Add a new section below with:
- Name (short, memorable)
- Prompt text (the STYLE line that goes into image prompts)
- Description (when to use it)
- Set `default: true` on ONE style

## Manga (Example)
- **Prompt:** Manga illustration, high contrast linework
- **Description:** Bold manga-style illustrations with clean black linework. Great for educational carousels with character-driven storytelling.
- **Reference:** See manga-style-guide.md for detailed rules
- default: true

## Cinematic Photo
- **Prompt:** Cinematic photography, shallow depth of field, warm color grading, natural lighting
- **Description:** Realistic photographic style. Professional, editorial feel. Good for personal brand carousels.

## Watercolor Editorial
- **Prompt:** Watercolor illustration, soft edges, muted earth tones, editorial style
- **Description:** Gentle, artistic feel. Good for reflective or emotional content.

## Minimalist Concept
- **Prompt:** Minimalist illustration, flat design, limited color palette, clean geometric shapes
- **Description:** Simple, modern, scannable. Good for tips and frameworks.

## Bold Graphic
- **Prompt:** Bold graphic design, saturated colors, strong typography integration, pop art influence
- **Description:** Eye-catching, high-energy. Good for hooks and attention-grabbing content.
