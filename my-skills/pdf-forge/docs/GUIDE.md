# PDF Forge — Your Document Design Studio

## One Sentence

PDF Forge turns your markdown into beautifully branded PDFs — one conversation, professional output.

## The Analogy

Think of PDF Forge like having a graphic designer on speed dial. You hand them your content (markdown), tell them which brand to use, and they hand back a polished document. Except this designer works instantly and never forgets your brand guidelines.

## What You Get

- **Cover page** with your logo and title (research reports)
- **Table of contents** that updates automatically
- **Your brand colours** on headers, tables, accents
- **Your fonts** throughout the document
- **Smart page breaks** that never split tables or orphan headings
- **Multiple styles** — research reports, guides, letters, workshop materials

## Two Brushes

PDF Forge is the structured brush — reliable, repeatable, brand-consistent. Set up your brand config once and every document matches.

The Frontend-Design skill (built by Anthropic) is the creative brush — for when you want something custom, beyond templates.

Use both. PDF Forge for your daily output. Frontend-Design when you want to get creative.

## The Mirror Pass

After generating a PDF, ask your Powerhouse to review it:

> "Review this PDF for layout issues, brand consistency, and any visual problems."

This catches mistakes before your client sees them. Produce → Mirror Pass → You review.

## When to Use

- Client session recaps
- Workshop workbooks
- Lead magnets and guides
- Monthly reports
- Onboarding welcome packs
- Research summaries
- Any document a client will see
