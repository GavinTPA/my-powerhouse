# Session Intelligence — How It Works

## The One-Sentence Version

After a Zoom session, trigger this skill and get two documents that tell you everything about who was there, what they said, and what they need next.

## Why This Exists

Every training call has hours of coaching intelligence buried in the chat and transcript. Who asked what. Who's struggling. Who's crushing it. Who was silent. What patterns emerged.

Most coaches never extract this because it takes hours of manual review. The insights die in a chat log no one reads.

Session Intelligence does the extraction automatically. You get two documents: one for the session (what happened), one for the people (who needs what).

## How You Use It

1. **After a Zoom session**, say "session intelligence" or "analyze this session"
2. **Answer 3-4 questions** — meeting type, Zoom profile, trainer names, business context
3. **Review the detected prompts** — the skill shows you what it found and lets you correct
4. **Get your documents** — Training Summary + Attendee Detail, saved to your vault

## What You Get

**Training Summary** — Hand this to a coach who missed the call. They'll know exactly what went down: session flow, key questions asked, trainer answers, coaching takeaways.

**Attendee Detail** — Every person who chatted gets a profile: their responses mapped to each prompt the trainer asked, plus a coaching note reading between the lines. People who were present but quiet get listed too.

## The Coaching Notes Are the Value

The skill doesn't just echo what people said. It synthesizes. If someone typed "3 clients, want 20" for current/target and "getting leads" for their gap — the coaching note connects those dots: "Lead generation is the bottleneck. 3 to 20 is aggressive — may need to sequence: ads first, then workshop, then conversion."

This is powered by your mission-context. The coaching notes use your business vocabulary, your frameworks, your stage language. Not generic advice.

## Works Without Prompts Too

Not every session uses chat prompts. Labs, hot seats, Q&A sessions — those are conversational. The skill detects this (zero prompts found) and switches to chronological mode: each person's chat contributions listed with timestamps, plus coaching notes based on what they asked and said.

## Requirements

- **zoom-connect** installed and configured with at least one profile
- **mission-context** set up with your business roles and vocabulary
- A Zoom session with cloud recording enabled (for chat log access)
