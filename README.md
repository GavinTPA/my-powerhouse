# My Powerhouse

Your personal AI-powered workspace. This is YOUR space — your context, your skills, your customizations.

## First Time Here?

Follow the Getting Started Guide (PDF) Ed sent you. If you've already completed the guide, here's the quick version:

1. Open your terminal
2. Navigate to this folder: `cd ~/Documents/my-powerhouse`
3. Launch Claude Code: `claude`
4. Say: "build my mission context"

## What's In Here

- **CLAUDE.md** — Your operating system. Claude reads this every session.
- **my-skills/** — Your AI tools (starts with mission-context-builder)
- **my-agents/** — Your workflow orchestrators (empty for now)
- **.claude/rules/** — Guardrails that prevent common mistakes

## Need Help?

Screenshot the problem + paste into Claude.ai or ChatGPT. See the troubleshooting method in the Tech Stack Setup Guide.
