Speaker bio

Gavin Smith gives business owners the tools and confidence to run their own numbers, without relying on an accountant.

He knows this world from both sides. Gavin is a qualified accountant who started at KPMG and rose to Global CFO of a $500M international marketing agency, overseeing finances across 60 countries. Then he walked away from the boardroom, moved back to the Sunshine Coast and became a business owner himself. Qualifications and all, running his own business humbled him fast.

He bought a bar turning over $1.1M and losing $150K a year, and grew it into a three-venue hospitality group now turning over more than $10M. He built and sold a chain of Pilates studios, and grew The Profit Analyst from startup to seven figures. Along the way he created his own simple financial operating system, built for business owners, not accountants, and has since coached more than 1,000 businesses across 40+ industries to use it in their own businesses.

Known for making numbers simple, practical and genuinely useful. No jargon. No theory. Audiences leave knowing what their numbers are telling them and exactly what to do next.

Talk topics
1

Profit and Cash Flow, Made Simple

How to understand and use your numbers

For owners who look at their reports and still don't know if the business is okay. Gavin cuts through the jargon to show what the P&L, balance sheet and cash flow are actually telling you, why profit doesn't always show up in the bank, and the simple weekly and monthly rhythm that keeps you in control at any size.

Walk away with the confidence to read your own numbers and act on them.
2

Boost Your Profit

The seven numbers that decide what you keep

The business has grown but profit hasn't. Gavin shows how The Profit Formula™ breaks any business into seven numbers, reveals where profit is leaking, and points to the one or two moves that will lift it fastest.

Walk away with your own profit formula and your next right move.
3

Maximise Your Exit

How to build a business worth selling, and get paid what it's worth

As a CFO, Gavin bought and sold businesses regularly. As an owner, he has exited businesses himself and is growing a hospitality group with a clear exit plan. This talk shows owners what buyers really pay for, and how to start building that value today, whether the exit is two years away or ten.

Walk away with the numbers that drive your sale price, and how to move them now.
