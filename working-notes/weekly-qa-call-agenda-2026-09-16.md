# Weekly Q&A Coaching Call — 2026-09-16 (Draft Agenda)

**Goal:** Give the regulars something genuinely new (not a repeat of hospitality or lead/lag indicators), close a couple of open loops from the last three calls, and protect time for the close so accountability doesn't get cut again.

**Context from reviewing Aug 26 / Sep 02 / Sep 09 calls:** same core group shows up every week and expects to learn something new each time. Last three calls leaned reactive — teaching topic got picked by whatever question landed rather than chosen in advance, and one topic regularly ate the whole call (Xero general ledger ran ~40 of 59 mins on Sep 02; cash-flow-priority topic ran so long on Sep 09 the planned close got cut). This draft timeboxes each section so that doesn't happen again.

---

## 1. Welcome & Highlights (8 min)

- Business or personal highlight from the week, drop in chat.
- Keep this tight — acknowledge each, go deeper on one or two max. This is rapport, not the lesson.

## 2. Pulse Check: Seven Levers from Monday's Workshop (7 min)

- Quick round: what lever did you pick as your Next Right Move, what's the first step you've taken.
- Not a deep dive — just accountability and a thread connecting this call back to the workshop.
- Note anyone stuck — flag for a 1:1 follow-up rather than solving live and eating group time.

## 3. New Teaching: Live Software Walkthrough (20 min)

- Everyone on this call is already a beta tester — use that. Screen-share the tool live.
- Walk through the actual workflow rather than describing it. Ask people to open theirs alongside you and try it in real time.
- Surface bugs/friction as they come up — that's useful data for you, not a distraction.
- This is the "something new" for the week — don't reach for hospitality or lead/lag indicators again, both already taught twice.

## 4. Open Floor / Carried-Over Questions (15 min)

Backup topics if there's room, in priority order:
- Close the loop with Liz on Xero COGS splitting into full product (raised Aug 26, half-answered Sep 02, never fully resolved).
- New framework not yet taught: accounts receivable / debtor days as a lead indicator for cash in the bank — relevant for Tate, Lindsey, Nick who invoice on terms.
- Genuine new questions from the group — ask directly rather than waiting for chat silence.

## 5. Wrap: Love / Learn / Next Action (10 min)

- What did you love, what did you learn — drop in chat.
- Everyone names one concrete next action from today's call (not "work on marketing" — the specific next step).
- Protect this time. If Section 3 or 4 runs long, cut there, not here.

---

**Timeboxes total: 60 min.** Set a visible timer for Section 3 so the software walkthrough doesn't become the whole call the way Xero did on Sep 02.
