# Monthly Profit Growth Workshop — 2026-09-10

**Goal:** Everyone leaves knowing their seven numbers, having filled in their Profit Calculator, and having picked one lever to focus on for the next 90 days.

**What's new this month:** Last month was about finding the seven numbers. This month is about treating each of those seven numbers as a lever the owner gets to choose to pull — not just a fact about the business. The one-pager (Seven Levers Playbook) gets handed out and worked live in the session.

---

## 1. Welcome & Frame (5 min)

- Why this workshop exists: this is the one skill that always tells you your next right move.
- Quick reminder: 80% is good enough. We're not doing tax returns, we're making decisions.
- Everyone needs their Clarity Report (last 3 months) to hand.

## 2. The Profit Formula Refresher (5 min)

- Income − Expenses = Profit. Not useful on its own.
- Zoom in one level: (Price × Volume) − Cost of Sales − Team − Marketing & Growth − Property − Overheads = Profit.
- Seven numbers. Know them off the top of your head and you can always see where you're winning and where you're losing.

## 3. Fill In Your Seven Numbers (15–20 min)

Work the worksheet live, easiest to hardest, same order as last time:

1. Overheads
2. Property / Rent
3. Marketing & Growth
4. Team
5. Cost of Sales
6. Price
7. Volume

- Three-month rolling average from the Clarity Report.
- Round to the thousand. If a number looks whack (one-off depreciation, EOY adjustment), swap it for a typical month instead of using it.
- Confirm everyone has all seven before moving on.

## 4. The Profit Calculator (10 min)

- Drop the seven numbers into the Profit Calculator.
- Read the baseline: current monthly profit, break-even point, break-even units.
- Look at the profitability table — what happens to profit as volume moves up.

## 5. The Seven Levers — Choosing Where to Focus (20–25 min)

This is the new part. Hand out the Seven Levers Playbook one-pager.

- Frame it: "These seven numbers aren't just a report card. They're seven decisions you get to make about where you put your time and money next."
- Run the Power of One: move each of the seven by just 1% and show the compounding effect on annual profit.
- Go lever by lever (or the top 2–3 if time is short) and put the playbook's questions to the room. For each lever, ask: is there an opportunity here, yes or no?
- Watch for the "I can't change this" answers (rent, fixed costs) — that's fine, the point is finding out which levers are actually live for each business, not forcing all seven.

## 6. Now vs Target (10 min)

- Everyone sets a target profit number (e.g. "what would an extra $100K a year look like per month?").
- Compare current profit formula vs target profit formula.
- Identify which 1–2 levers from step 5 would do most of the work to close that gap. That's the Next Right Move.

## 7. Wrap & Next Steps (5 min)

Recap what everyone leaves with:

- Their seven numbers
- A filled Profit Calculator
- The Seven Levers Playbook to keep working the questions after today
- One Next Right Move to focus on for the next 90 days

Tie to the monthly rhythm: next month we compare actuals against the target formula set today and check off progress on the lever chosen.
