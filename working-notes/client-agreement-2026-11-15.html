Client agreement
Terms of your agreement

This agreement is between you (the business named on page 1) and The Profit Analyst Australia Pty Ltd (ABN 83 651 793 449) ("we" or "us").

1. Your 90 days

1.1Your 90 days start on the date of your Onboarding Call.

1.2Please book your Onboarding Call within 14 days of signing. If it hasn't happened within 14 days of signing, your 90 days start on day 14 anyway.

1.3We need read access to your accounting software to install your tools. Delays in giving us access don't extend your 90 days.

1.4Missed calls aren't carried forward, but recordings of group calls are available in the software.

2. Fees and payments

2.1You pay the program fee by the payment option ticked on page 1. All payments are by direct debit or card, collected automatically.

2.2If you leave the program early, any remaining payments under your payment option are still payable, except as set out in clause 4. You've reserved a place and your tools are installed in the first 14 days.

2.3If a payment fails, we'll let you know and try again. If it's still unpaid after 7 days, we may pause your access to calls and the software until it's paid.

2.4Each extra company in your group with its own books (Xero, MYOB or similar) gets its own Profit Tools at $300 + GST per month, billed monthly from signing, during and after your 90 days, whatever your payment option. Remove one with 30 days' written notice.

3. What happens after your 90 days

3.1When your 90 days end, your support continues automatically on Profit Coaching at $900 + GST per month, so there's no gap in your tools or coaching.

3.2Billing starts the day after your 90 days end, and repeats on that date each month.

3.3About 2 weeks before your 90 days end, we'll email you to confirm what happens next, the date of your first monthly payment, and how to change it.

3.4You can choose instead to move to Profit Tools at $300 + GST per month, move up to Profit Partner at $1,500 + GST per month (if a place is available), or stop. Just reply to that email or tell us in writing before your 90 days end.

3.5There's no lock-in. After day 91, you can change your option or cancel any time with 30 days' written notice. Email is fine.

3.6We may change monthly prices with at least 30 days' written notice. You can cancel before the new price applies.

4. The Profit & Cash Flow Guarantee

4.1At your 90-Day Roadmap Call, we'll do two things:

  • map out a clear plan, using your Profit Calculator™, showing what your numbers need to look like to add $100,000 in annual profit
  • check your Weekly Cash Flow Forecaster™ to make sure your business can pay the remaining program payments when they're due

4.2If either answer is no, or we don't believe we can help you win, we'll stop the program at that point. You won't owe any remaining payments, and you keep your 90-Day Roadmap and your forecast. If you paid in full, we'll refund two monthly payments ($6,000 + GST).

4.3The guarantee applies when you have:

  • given us access to your accounting software within 7 days of signing
  • attended your Onboarding Call
  • attended your 90-Day Roadmap Call with Gavin

4.4The profit plan shows what your numbers need to look like. How quickly you get there, and whether you act on it, is up to you, as set out in clause 6.

4.5Apart from clause 4.2 and your rights under Australian Consumer Law, payments already made are non-refundable.

5. Our materials

5.1Your financial data stays yours. We only use it to deliver the program, and we keep it confidential.

5.2The Profit Analyst Method™, the training, the software and our templates belong to us. You can use them in your own business, but you can't share, copy or resell them.

5.3Please don't share call recordings or other members' information outside the program.

6. Results and responsibility

6.1We teach you to understand and use your numbers. Every decision in your business is yours to make, and your results depend on the actions you take.

6.2We don't give tax, legal, accounting or financial product advice. Your accountant still handles compliance and tax.

6.3To the extent the law allows, our total liability to you is limited to the fees you've paid us in the previous 12 months.

7. How we treat each other

7.1Be respectful to our team and other members on calls and in messages.

7.2If someone is disrespectful or disruptive, we may end their agreement with written notice and refund any unused part of what they've paid.

8. General

8.1This agreement, including page 1 and the Promise, is the whole agreement between us.

8.2Notices and changes must be in writing. Email counts.

8.3If any part of this agreement can't be enforced, the rest still applies.

8.4This agreement is governed by the laws of Queensland, Australia.

By signing, you agree to the Promise and these terms, including that your support continues on Profit Coaching at $900 + GST per month after your 90 days unless you tell us otherwise.
