Weekly: Track

Every Monday · 10 mins

"Are we okay?"

  • Check your key numbers so you know the business is on track
  • Know your cash position and when your next low point is coming
  • See how much cash is free for your next growth move

Using your Weekly Cash Flow Forecaster and Dashboard

Monthly: Understand

Each month · 30 mins

"How did we go, and why?"

  • Are we making a profit? Yes or no, and by how much
  • Where are we winning and losing? What's really happening in the business
  • Where is the profit going and how are we funding growth?
  • What's our financial position? A clear handle on what we own and owe

Using your Monthly Clarity Report

Quarterly: Plan

Each quarter · 2 hours

"Where are we going, and what's our next sprint?"

Look back
  • A clear snapshot of where the business is and how it's performing
  • Strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and threats
  • Wins and lessons from the last quarter
Look forward
  • Tie the numbers to your goals
  • A financial roadmap from today to five years out
  • Use the numbers to set the next quarter's priorities
