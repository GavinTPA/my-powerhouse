# Monthly Profit Workshop: 2026-10-08

**Goal:** Everyone leaves with their seven numbers (Now), a Target formula, and one lever they're working on this month.

**What changes this month and why:** In August and September, filling in the numbers plus the calculator login took most of the hour. The Target column on the handout never got filled in, even though it was planned in September. Both calls ran over (10 min and 4 min). So this month:
- The one-pager does most of the teaching, which cuts the intro to 7 minutes.
- The calculator is demo-only on your screen. They log in afterwards as homework.
- Target and the next right move get a protected 20 minutes.

**Send before the call:** Handout slides, Profit Formula one-pager, Profit Playbook. Ask people to have their Clarity Report open (calendar-year version, last 3 months of actuals).

---

## 1. Welcome and set-up (0:00 to 0:03)
- Clarity Report open (calendar-year version), handout and one-pager ready. A pen and paper works too.
- Chat codes for today: one letter per number as they finish it (O, P, M, T, C).
- Jade/Melinda handle "where do I find it" questions in the chat.

## 2. Why the formula matters (0:03 to 0:10) *hard stop*
- Finance Ecosystem: today is only the profit circle. Cash flow is there to fund the moves.
- Profit is the outcome, the formula is the recipe. Seven numbers.
- Two jobs: **know your business** (read each month's results against your seven) and **find your next right move** (each number is a lever).
- Four principles, using the one-pager strip: round to thousands, 3-month average, skip outliers, fill everything in first and talk about what it means after.

## 3. Find your NOW (0:10 to 0:28)
Work backwards, easiest first, exactly as the one-pager table runs:
1. Overheads $ → 2. Property $ → 3. Marketing & Growth $ (ask everyone to type their actual number, it's a good conversation starter) → 4. Team $ → 5. Cost of Sales % (from P&L ratios, not dollars)
6. Total revenue, 3-month average (written in the margin)
7. Pick price OR volume, divide to get the other.
- **Parking lot:** when someone jumps ahead to "what does this mean / should I cut X", say "great, hold that for Target" and keep moving. This happened in both previous calls and cost time.

## 4. Check it and tell the story (0:28 to 0:33)
- Run the formula: does it land near your average operating profit? If it's way off, check Cost of Sales % and Team first.
- Ask one volunteer to tell their profit story in one sentence (use the bar example on the one-pager as the model).
- A loss is fine. You use the same tool to get back to profit.

## 5. Set your TARGET (0:33 to 0:50) *protect this part*
- Default target: +$100k a year = **+$8.3k a month**. People can pick their own number.
- **Power of one demo (3 min, your screen only):** 1% better on all seven, then show which levers moved profit most. Takeaway: price and volume first, then your biggest cost.
- For each number, ask: can I change it, or not? Cross off the ones that won't move (usually rent, often overheads).
- Pick the top 2 levers and ask the Profit Playbook questions of those two only, out loud.
- Fill in the Target row so the formula adds up to the target profit.

## 6. Commit to the next right move (0:50 to 0:56)
Everyone types in chat:
> My lever is ___. My one action this month is ___.

Give quick reactions. Push anyone who picked a lever that can't move toward price, volume or their biggest cost.

## 7. Wrap (0:56 to 1:00)
- Rhythm: monthly, compare actual results to the Target formula; quarterly, reset it.
- Homework: put both Now and Target into the Profit Calculator (link + password in chat).
- Next touchpoint: the monthly financial review checks this month's result against today's Target formula.

## If time runs short
Cut in this order: the volunteer story in section 4, then the Playbook questions (send them away with the Playbook instead). Never cut section 6.

## Watch-outs from past calls
- **Seasonal businesses (MJ/Tom):** fill in a peak formula and a quiet-months formula.
- **Lumpy pricing (trades, BSL):** revenue ÷ invoices is fine. The numbers matter less than the questions you ask about them.
- **June outliers:** year-end depreciation or catch-up journals. Leave them out of the average.
- **New people:** Clarity Report is the PDF with two pages of numbers and colour charts, not the Cash Flow Forecaster.
