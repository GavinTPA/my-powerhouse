## Prioritized & Owned Roadmap (2026-07-19)

Context: 2 days out from the Boardroom event in Thailand, mid-year (July 19), feeling burnt out with too many projects and not enough visible progress, plus a hard 5-6 week clock on replacing Hannah. This roadmap deliberately narrows the 10-intention project list down to a small active set, sequenced by what unlocks the most and what can't wait — everything else is parked on purpose, not forgotten.

### Tier 0 — Ticking clock, running now regardless of everything else
- **Recruit and onboard Hannah's replacement** — Owner: Gavin, with Team support on screening/logistics. Why now: 5-6 week runway, non-negotiable.

### Tier 1 — Active this quarter (the only things to actively push on)
1. **Niche-decision sprint** — Owner: Gavin & Min jointly. Why: the single highest-leverage unlock — it directly feeds the message rewrite, stage-booking criteria, and repricing work queued below. Recommend running this live at Boardroom rather than back home — get the room's outside perspective on it.
2. **Cost/expense audit** — Owner: Team (offshore delivery team pulls the numbers) + Gavin reviews and decides cuts. Why: fast, low-effort, and gives real numbers on the cash-flow stress instead of guessing.
3. **Client onboarding/education redesign** — Owner: Gavin, with Kristen for content support. Why: directly targets "clients don't value the system, just want Gav" — the single biggest constraint on scaling past Gavin's own time.
4. **Boardroom engagement — go in with a plan** — Owner: Gavin & Min. Why: the event is in 2 days; this is about getting the most out of it, not building something new.

### Tier 2 — Queued next (once Tier 1 lands, likely next quarter)
- Rewrite marketing assets to the single message — Min (waits on the niche decision)
- Stage-booking criteria — Min (waits on the niche decision)
- Repricing/offer-ladder redesign — Gavin & Min (waits on the niche decision)
- Structured client check-in cadence + client handoff process to the team — Gavin & Team (natural follow-on to the onboarding redesign)
- Hero-to-MRR rollover redesign (contract + sales-ops automation) — Gavin & Min (better tackled once Hannah's replacement is settled)

### Parked — deliberately not now
- Marketing strategist hire, channel audit/testing — needs a clearer budget picture from the cost audit first
- GavBot/software finish and launch, book publishing — big lifts, and worth naming out loud: this is the same pattern as "Gav starts projects and doesn't finish them." Don't start pushing on these again until Tier 0/1 has actually landed.
- Financial storytelling content series, podcast relaunch, personal brand refresh — energizing but discretionary; pick back up when there's real capacity, not out of guilt
- Usage-tracking dashboard, internal profit dashboard, individual team development plans — good infrastructure, not urgent enough to compete with the above

### What to mastermind at Boardroom
- The niche/positioning decision — get outside perspective from operators who've solved the "everyone needs it so we sell to everyone" trap
- Hitting 25% profit margin with the current wage burden — a concrete numbers problem other 7-figure owners can speak to directly
- Team structure for delivery beyond Gav, especially replacing Hannah — ask for playbooks or referrals from peers who've solved founder-dependency in their own delivery teams
- Worth naming to the room, if it feels right: the burnout itself. Boardroom peers who've been through a similar stretch may be the most useful input of the whole event.
